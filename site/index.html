Birding Wrapped

Your year in birds, as story cards.

Drop in your eBird data and get your new species, the countries they came from, your signature bird and your birding personality, as cards sized for Instagram Stories. Nothing is uploaded. Your file is read in this browser tab only.

Where do I get the file?

Either of these eBird downloads works.

  1. Your life list. On eBird, open My eBird, then Life List, then Download (CSV). You get one row per species with the date you first saw it, so the cards show your new species by year.
  2. All your data. Sign in at ebird.org/downloadMyData and press Submit. eBird emails you a zip with MyEBirdData.csv. This adds hours in the field, checklists, your most seen bird and your usual start time.

Other apps work too if their export has a species column and a date column.

Or paste a list of birds

One bird per line, common or scientific name. Add a date after a comma if you know it, like Blue Jay, 2025-04-12. Birds with no date count in .

Tip: tap any bird on a card to see its details.

Like your cards? Share them and tag @himanshuguptapro on Instagram. I'd love to see your year.

Share cards

Instagram Stories takes one image at a time. To post several, save them to your photos from the share sheet first, then add them in Instagram one by one.
